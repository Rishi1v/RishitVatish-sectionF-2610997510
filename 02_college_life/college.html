College Terminology (Description List)

Semester
One half of the academic year, usually about six months long.
Attendance
The percentage of lectures a student has attended.
Practical
A lab session where we apply what we learn in theory.
Assignment
Work given by a teacher that has to be submitted before a deadline.
CGPA
Cumulative Grade Point Average, the overall score of a student.
