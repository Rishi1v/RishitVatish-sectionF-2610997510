India is a land of colours, cultures and contrasts. From the snow-covered Himalayas in the north to the golden beaches of Goa and the backwaters of Kerala, every state has its own story. You can walk through ancient forts and palaces, taste street food on every corner, and meet people who will make you feel at home. No matter what kind of traveller you are, India has something for you.
